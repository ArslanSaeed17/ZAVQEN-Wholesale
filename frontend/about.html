Who we are

ZAVQEN Wholesale is a single-owner wholesale store built for buyers who order in bulk — boutique owners, retailers, and resellers who need consistent stock without negotiating a fresh quote every time. Everything listed is real, in-stock inventory with a set minimum order quantity and a fixed unit price, so what you see is what you pay.

How it works

Browse the catalog, add items to your cart at or above each product's MOQ, and check out with cash on delivery or bank transfer. Every order is validated server-side against live stock before it's confirmed, so there are no surprises after you've placed it.

Why buyers choose us

No account managers to chase, no minimum-order guesswork, and no waiting on a quote email. Just a catalog, clear pricing, and an order history you can track from your dashboard.
