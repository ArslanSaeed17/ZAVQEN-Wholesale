Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before placing a bulk order.

What does MOQ mean?

MOQ stands for Minimum Order Quantity — the smallest amount of a product you can order. It's shown on every product card and product page, and the cart automatically enforces it.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash on Delivery (COD) and Bank Transfer. You'll choose your preferred method at checkout.

How do I track my order?

Log in and open your Dashboard — every order shows its current status (Pending, Processing, Shipped, Delivered, or Cancelled).

Can I change my order after placing it?

Reach out to us via WhatsApp or the Contact page as soon as possible with your order number. Once an order has shipped, changes usually aren't possible.

What if an item is out of stock?

Out-of-stock items are clearly marked and can't be added to cart. Stock is checked again at checkout, so quantities are always accurate at the time you order.

Do you ship across Pakistan?

Yes — we ship nationwide. Delivery timelines vary by city and are confirmed once your order moves to "Shipped."

Is my account information secure?

Yes. Authentication is handled by Supabase with email verification, and every account can only ever see its own orders and addresses — enforced at the database level, not just in the app.
