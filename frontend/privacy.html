Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

What we collect

When you register, we collect your full name, email, and phone number. When you place an order, we collect the shipping details you provide (name, phone, address). We never ask for or store payment card details — Cash on Delivery and Bank Transfer are handled outside this website.

How we use it

Your information is used to create and manage your account, process and deliver your orders, and respond to messages you send us. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

How it's stored

Account and order data is stored in our Supabase database, protected by Row Level Security — technical rules enforced by the database itself that ensure your orders and addresses are only ever visible to you and to store administrators, regardless of how the request is made.

Cookies

We use only the minimum browser storage needed to keep you signed in between visits (your session token). We don't use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

Your rights

You can review and update your profile and saved addresses at any time from your Dashboard. To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact us using the details on our Contact page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through our Contact page.
